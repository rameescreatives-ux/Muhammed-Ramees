How much does branding cost in the UAE in 2026?

Every agency in this market answers that question with "it depends". Here are the actual numbers — what a logo costs, what a full identity costs, why the range is so wide, and where a new business should genuinely spend.

Muhammed Ramees Updated October 2026 11 min read

The short answer

  • Logo only: AED 500–3,000 freelance, AED 2,000–6,000 from a small studio, AED 8,000–25,000 from an agency.
  • Logo + brand guidelines: AED 3,000–9,000 from a studio, AED 15,000–45,000 from an agency.
  • Full identity with strategy, naming and rollout: AED 12,000–40,000 from a studio, AED 50,000–250,000 from a large agency.
  • Adding a website: AED 4,000–15,000 for a brochure site, AED 20,000+ for e-commerce.
  • Most UAE startups spend AED 2,000–6,000 on their first identity, and that is a reasonable place to start.

If you have asked three agencies in Dubai what a brand identity costs, you have probably had three versions of the same non-answer: it depends on scope, we would need to understand your business first, let us set up a call. That is not entirely dishonest — scope genuinely does change the number — but it is also a sales technique. The longer a price stays hidden, the longer you stay in a conversation.

So here is the information that conversation is designed to delay. These are real 2026 numbers for the UAE market, based on what studios and agencies here actually quote.

What a logo costs in the UAE

A logo on its own is the cheapest thing you can buy and the easiest to get wrong, because the price range is enormous and the quality difference is not always visible until you try to use it.

Who you hireTypical priceWhat you usually get
Fiverr / marketplace AED 75–500 A PNG and maybe a vector. Often a modified stock mark. No usage rules, no variations, frequently not legally yours.
Local freelancer AED 500–3,000 A logo and files. Quality swings widely. Source files sometimes withheld.
Small studio AED 2,000–6,000 Logo system with variations, colour and type, a short guide, source files.
Mid-size agency AED 8,000–25,000 The same deliverables with a strategy phase and a formal presentation.
Large / international agency AED 40,000+ Research, workshops, multiple routes, a team of people, and an account manager between you and them.

The jump from AED 500 to AED 5,000 is not mostly about how the logo looks. It is about whether you receive a system — a mark that works at 16 pixels and on a building, lockups for horizontal and vertical space, a defined palette, and permission to actually own all of it.

What makes a cheap logo expensive later

  • No vector file. A PNG cannot go on a sign, a vehicle or a box. You will pay someone to redraw it.
  • One version only. No horizontal lockup, no single-colour version, nothing that works on a dark background. Every new application becomes a small job.
  • No ownership transfer. Marketplace sellers often licence rather than assign. If you cannot register it as a trademark, it is not your brand.
  • Unlicensed fonts. A logo built on a font the designer did not licence for commercial use is a liability you inherit.

What a full brand identity costs

"Brand identity" means the logo plus everything that makes it usable: colour, typography, imagery direction, graphic devices, and the document that tells people how to apply them.

ScopeStudio priceAgency price
Logo + mini guide (4–6 pages)AED 2,000–4,000AED 10,000–20,000
Logo + full brand book (20–40 pages)AED 3,000–9,000AED 15,000–45,000
Identity + naming + verbal identityAED 8,000–18,000AED 40,000–120,000
Full programme with research and rolloutAED 12,000–40,000AED 60,000–250,000

The honest version

For most first-time UAE businesses, the difference between a AED 3,000 identity and a AED 30,000 identity is not the quality of the final logo. It is the amount of research, the number of stakeholders being managed, and the number of people the fee has to pay. A two-person studio can deliver the same finished artwork because there is no account layer, no new-business team and no office in DIFC inside the price.

Where the big fee genuinely earns its money: multi-market brands, regulated industries, naming that needs legal clearance in several jurisdictions, and organisations where fifteen people have to agree.

What drives the price up

Six things move the number more than anything else.

  1. Bilingual Arabic and English. Done properly this is two pieces of lettering drawn to match in weight, rhythm and balance — not an Arabic font dropped under the English. Expect 30–50% on top of a single-script identity, and be suspicious of anyone who charges nothing extra.
  2. Naming. Generating and screening names is slow, and most candidates die at the trademark or domain check. Naming alone runs AED 5,000–30,000.
  3. Number of applications. A logo is one artwork. Packaging across nine SKUs, a vehicle livery, signage and a uniform are nine, and each needs its own production file.
  4. Stakeholders. Every additional decision-maker adds rounds. A founder deciding alone is genuinely cheaper than a committee, and good studios price for that.
  5. Research depth. Interviews, category audits and customer research are real work. They are also optional for a business that has not launched yet.
  6. Speed. A two-week deadline on a six-week project is a rush fee everywhere, usually 25–50%.

What a startup can safely skip

If you are pre-launch or in your first year, several standard agency line items are genuinely postponable.

  • A 60-page brand book. If three people will apply the brand, a tight 15–20 pages is better than a volume nobody opens.
  • Deep market research. Before you have customers, you are researching assumptions. Launch, learn, then invest in research when it can change something.
  • A motion identity. Lovely, and you can add it the quarter you start running video ads.
  • Multiple naming rounds. If the name is already decided and registered on the trade licence, do not reopen it.

What is not safely skippable:

  • Vector source files you own outright.
  • A logo that works in one colour, on dark, and at small sizes.
  • Defined colour values in HEX, RGB, CMYK and Pantone — your printer will ask.
  • Licensed fonts, or fonts that are free for commercial use.

A brand you cannot afford to apply is worse than a simpler brand you can use everywhere.

This is the most common expensive mistake we see: a founder spends their whole budget on an identity, then has nothing left for packaging, signage or a site. The brand lives in a PDF. Split the budget so there is always enough to put the work in front of customers.

How to compare two quotes properly

When quotes differ by 5x, the gap is almost always in these eight lines. Ask about each one before you decide.

  1. How many initial directions, and how many revision rounds are included?
  2. Do I receive editable source files — AI, INDD — or only exported PNG and PDF?
  3. Is full ownership assigned to me in writing?
  4. Are the fonts licensed for commercial use, and who pays for the licence?
  5. What exactly is in the guidelines, and how many pages?
  6. Is an Arabic lockup included or quoted separately?
  7. Who is actually doing the work — the person in this meeting, or someone else?
  8. What happens, and what does it cost, if I need a small change in six months?

A studio that answers all eight in a sentence each is usually the safer hire, whatever the number at the bottom of the page.

Frequently asked

Is AED 500 for a logo ever acceptable?

For testing an idea you may abandon in three months, yes. For a business you have put a trade licence behind, no — mostly because of the ownership and font-licence problems, which are cheap to avoid now and expensive to fix after you have printed packaging.

Why are Dubai agencies more expensive than Abu Dhabi ones?

Mostly overhead and market positioning rather than skill. Dubai has more agencies competing for the same enterprise clients, which raises both office costs and price expectations. The design talent is distributed across both.

Should I pay for branding before I have customers?

Pay for enough to look credible and launch — a proper logo, a colour and type system, and consistent application. Postpone the deep strategic work until you have real customer evidence to base it on.

Do prices include VAT?

Usually not. UAE VAT is 5% and most studios quote excluding it. Always confirm, because on a AED 40,000 project that is AED 2,000 you did not budget for.
