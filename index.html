Brand Identity Arabic + English F&B

Salkaara — Arabic Cuisine

Restaurant brand · Full identity system

A charcoal-grill restaurant opening into a category where almost every competitor uses the same red-and-orange template. The brief was warmth and heritage without looking dated.

The wordmark takes its sweeping terminals from Arabic calligraphy while staying readable to English speakers, and a second lockup renders the same name in an Arabic-styled form so both markets get a mark that feels native rather than translated. A skewer hidden in the rising steam does the storytelling that an obvious grill illustration would have flattened.

Heidi — Hair and Glow

Hair & skincare brand · Identity and packaging

A natural hair and skincare line launching into a category where every shelf already looks botanical. Green and a leaf are table stakes; the job was making them feel expensive.

The answer was restraint plus one gold accent — a rounded, soft wordmark for the femininity and warmth the category expects, a leaf doing the natural signal quietly, and a glow mark on the "i" that carries the second half of the name. Deep forest green became the background rather than the logo, so the gold reads as luxury instead of decoration across bottles, droppers and cartons.

FreshRoutes International

UAE produce distributor · Identity and 8-page profile

A new UAE fresh-produce distributor selling to supermarkets, hotels and wholesalers — buyers who decide from a document before they ever meet you.

FAQs

Questions GCC founders ask.

How much does a logo cost in Abu Dhabi?

Our Launch package is AED 1,999 and includes the primary logo, three lockup variations, a colour and type system, a four-page mini guide and the source files. Across the UAE market generally you will see anything from AED 500 from a freelancer to AED 25,000 from a large agency — the difference is usually strategy, documentation and whether you receive editable files at the end.

How long does a branding project take?

Launch takes 7–10 days. Identity takes two to three weeks. Complete, which includes a website, takes four to six weeks. The main thing that slows a project down is waiting on feedback, so we agree review dates at the start.

Do you design Arabic logos as well as English?

Yes, and we draw them together rather than translating afterwards. An Arabic lockup needs matching weight, matching rhythm and a different sense of balance — dropping an Arabic font under an English wordmark is visibly wrong to any Arabic reader. Bilingual lockups are included from the Identity package upward.

Do you work with clients outside the UAE?

Yes. We are based in Abu Dhabi and work remotely across Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain and further afield. Everything runs over calls, shared files and WhatsApp, and we reply at whatever hour suits your timezone.

Do I get the editable source files?

Always, at every tier, with no extra charge. That means the working Illustrator and InDesign files, not just flattened PNGs and PDFs. Some studios hold source files back so you have to come back to them for every small change. We think that is a bad way to treat a client.

Can you rebrand an existing business?

Yes. The first job is working out what is worth protecting — a colour your customers recognise, a name with real equity — before we change anything. Sometimes the answer is a full repositioning; often it is a sharper, better-built version of what you already have.

What do you need from me to start?

The business name, a sense of who your customer is, and examples of brands you like and dislike. That is genuinely enough for a first call. If you have a trade licence, a product list or competitor names, those help, but do not wait until everything is ready.

How do payments work?

Fifty per cent to begin and fifty per cent on final handover, by bank transfer. Prices on this page exclude VAT. Two rounds of revisions are included at every tier, and anything beyond that is quoted before the work happens — never billed as a surprise.
